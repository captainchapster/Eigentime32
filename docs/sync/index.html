Sync with this phone

One tap sets the full date, time and time zone, to within a few milliseconds.

  1. Open the watch lid so you can see the result.
  2. Tap Start sync.
  3. Hold the back of the phone flat on the watch face. The NFC spot is usually near the camera.
  4. Keep still until the phone buzzes and this page says Synced.

Sync with the buttons

Fine-tune with the buttons

Works with any phone or clock. It corrects the seconds, up to half a minute either way, and still teaches the watch its drift.

  1. Open the lid and hold the 2 o'clock button for a second, until the face shows PRESS AT :00.
  2. Watch the bar above. When the seconds reach :00, press the 2 o'clock button once.
  3. The face shows ADJ +1.2S, which is how far off the watch was.

If the hour or date is wrong, or the face says NOT SET, set it by hand first (below), then do this.

Set the time by hand
  1. Hold the 10 o'clock button for a second. The hour is highlighted.
  2. Press 2 o'clock to count up. Hold it to go faster.
  3. Press 10 o'clock to move on: hour, minute, day, month, year. After the year it saves and shows TIME SET.

Hold 10 o'clock at any point to cancel. A hand-set time isn't precise enough to measure drift, so follow it with a sync.

How syncing keeps it accurate

A phone sync sends one number: the phone's network time in milliseconds since 1970, which pins down the date, time and seconds, plus your time zone. The watch sets its clock on the next whole second.

Before it does, it measures how far it had drifted since the last sync. If that was at least a week ago, it works out its rate error and trims its own crystal to cancel it. Each sync makes it better, so a sync every few months is plenty.

Tapping any phone on the watch, even an iPhone, opens this page with the watch's latest status.
