Sync with a tap

Sets the full date, time and time zone in one go.

  1. Open the watch lid, so you can see the result.
  2. Tap Sync the watch below.
  3. Hold the back of the phone flat against the watch face and keep it still until this page says Synced. It takes about a second.

Set the date and time with the buttons

Works with any phone. Use this if the watch shows no time, for example after a battery change.

  1. Open the lid. Hold A for a second until the hour starts flashing.
  2. Press B to change the flashing number. Hold B to run it up quickly.
  3. Press A to move on: hour, minute, day, month, year.
  4. When the year is right, press A once more to save. The watch shows TIME SET, with the seconds at :00. Then fine-tune the seconds as below.

To back out without saving, hold A. The watch shows CANCELLED.

Fine-tune the seconds

For a watch that's already set: lines it up with this phone to a fraction of a second, and over time teaches it its own rate. It only corrects up to 30 seconds and never changes the date.

  1. Open the lid. Hold B until the face shows PRESS AT :00.
  2. Watch the clock at the top of this page. Press B exactly as the seconds reach :00.
  3. The watch snaps to that minute and shows how far off it was, e.g. ADJ -0.8S.

To back out, press A.
