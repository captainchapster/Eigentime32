This phone's clock (network time)
Tap Sync, then hold the phone to the front of the watch until this page says Synced.
Sync with the buttons (any phone)
  1. Open the lid and hold the 2 o'clock button until the face shows PRESS AT :00.
  2. Watch the clock above and press the 2 o'clock button when the seconds reach :00.
The watch snaps to the nearest minute and, after a week or more since the last sync, trims its own rate.
