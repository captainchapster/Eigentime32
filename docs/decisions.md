# Decisions

| Date | Decision | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 2026-10-10 | RV-3032-C7 TCXO RTC does all timekeeping; MCU sleeps | ±1.5 ppm at ~160 nA, offset register for drift trim |
| 2026-10-10 | Sharp LS013B7DH03 memory LCD, on only while lid is open | Microwatt power, sunlight-readable; in production (~33,700 at DigiKey) |
| 2026-10-10 | 3D-printed case, designed around the PCB | Build around the device, not the reverse |
| 2026-10-10 | Round PCB, optimised for compactness | LCD diagonal (40.3 mm) sets the diameter: 40 mm PCB, ~44 mm case |
| 2026-10-10 | TPS61099 boost at 3.0 V for the LCD | Keeps the LCD in spec as the cell sags |
| 2026-10-10 | TPS22916C load switch ahead of the TPS61099 (confirmed by Alex) | Takes the boost's 0.5–1.6 µA shutdown current and the 100 µF bulk's leakage off the cell while the lid is shut |
| 2026-10-10 | 470 Ω inrush resistor after the load switch; 1M/470 nF delay on the boost EN | The switch's slew alone would pull 220–350 mA from the coin cell; the delay lets the bulk charge before the boost starts (~0.3 s to display power) |
| 2026-10-10 | Keep the NFC tag: ST25DV04KC-IE6C3 (UFDFPN8); the ST25DV04K is NRND | Yearly phone recalibration controls crystal aging; GPO works from RF power, so a phone tap wakes the MCU |
| 2026-10-10 | CR2032 in a Keystone 3034 SMT retainer (4.06 mm) instead of CR2450 | ~8.4 mm total thickness; still ~2× capacity margin at ten years |
| 2026-10-10 | DMG2305UX P-FET reverse protection, gate to GND | Fully on at 1.8 V gate drive (200 mΩ max); off with a reversed cell |
| 2026-10-10 | STM32L031G6U6 (UFQFPN28) as the MCU | 19 usable I/O after SWD; pin map in power-pins-accuracy.md §5 |
| 2026-10-10 | Lid sensor: Allegro CT8132SK-IS3 TMR switch (push-pull, no pull-up) instead of an NC reed | SMD normally-closed reeds are Form C parts ~27 mm long (Littelfuse MDSM-DT) and don't fit; the Coto RR122 is obsolete. Costs 190 nA typ / 900 nA max average |
| 2026-10-10 | Buttons: Panasonic EVQ-P7A01P side-push, 1.35 mm high, actuators at the board edge; 1 MΩ external pull-ups, internal pull-ups off | Operated through the case wall; a held button costs 3 µA, not ~75 µA |
| 2026-10-10 | Case orientation (front view): buttons at ~10 and ~2 o'clock, hinge at 9, lid magnet / sensor at ~4, LCD FPC at 6, CR2032 slides in from 12 | Lid sensor roughly opposite the hinge; the FPC leaves the glass at 6 o'clock and folds under the panel |
| 2026-10-10 | Keep a 21 mm wide no-parts channel from the holder to the 12 o'clock edge (rule area on the back) | The Keystone 3034 is a tunnel retainer with ~0.5 mm headroom over the cell, so the CR2032 slides in flat and its trailing edge starts beyond the board edge: the case needs a slot at 12 o'clock, or the board comes out for a swap |
| 2026-10-10 | NFC coil: 8-turn F.Cu spiral, 38.1 mm OD, 0.15/0.15 mm, terminals at 4-5 o'clock | 0.9 mm under the planned 39 mm so the outer terminal via fits inside the 0.3 mm edge clearance; ~5.1 uH free air |
| 2026-10-10 | CR2032 negative-contact pad reduced from the KiCad footprint's 17.8 mm to 5 mm (project footprint) | Keystone p.11 asks for 0.156 in (3.96 mm) square minimum; frees the area under the cell for mask-covered tracks |
| 2026-10-10 | Board rules: 0.127 mm clearance, 0.2 mm signal / 0.25 mm power tracks, 0.45/0.3 mm vias (0.05 mm min ring), 0.2 mm hole-to-copper, 0.3 mm copper-to-edge | Inside JLCPCB's no-surcharge 4-layer limits (0.3 mm hole with 0.4/0.45 mm pad is the free via tier; 0.2 mm holes add ~$37 with a forced Kelvin test and Tg155) |
| 2026-10-10 | 4 layers, 0.8 mm, JLCPCB JLC04081H-3313 (layout-4l branch): 35 um Cu / 3313 prepreg 0.0994 mm / 15.2 um Cu / core 0.5 mm / 15.2 um Cu / 3313 prepreg 0.0994 mm / 35 um Cu. In1/In2 are signal layers, no planes | 2 layers stalled at ~20 unrouted. Impedance doesn't matter here. JLCPCB quote 2026-10-10: $8 for 5 boards (40x40 mm, HASL, 3-4 day build) plus shipping |
| 2026-10-10 | No tracks or vias on F.Cu/In1/In2 between r 16.4 and 19.1 mm (coil ring); B.Cu allowed | The coil spiral is on F.Cu, so a via there would short it; parts in the ring can only escape on B.Cu |
| 2026-10-10 | I2C moved from PB6/PB7 to PA9/PA10 (both I2C1) | PA9/PA10 face the RTC/NFC side of the MCU; PB6/PB7 stay free with USART2 TX/RX for a debug UART |
| 2026-10-10 | No copper pour anywhere on the board | The coil encloses the whole board; a ground pour inside the loop would kill the NFC coupling |
| 2026-10-10 | LCD inputs go through VLCD-powered buffers | LCD VIH is VDD − 0.1 V; the MCU on VBAT (down to ~2.2 V) can't meet it at VLCD 3.0 V |
| 2026-10-10 | SWD on Tag-Connect TC2030-NL pads | No connector height; smallest footprint |
| 2026-10-10 | LCD FPC folds 180 deg under the glass (Sharp LD-26X06A Fig. 8-2) into J2 (Panasonic Y5B AYF531035, Sharp's recommended part) on the PCB FRONT, under the panel. Panel stays centred (decided by Alex, 2026-10-10) | Folding round the 6 o'clock board edge put the bend ~4.3-8 mm from the glass, across the tail's width step (~5.6-6.1 mm) and outside Sharp's 0.8-6.0 mm bend zone. Keeping it in the zone that way needed a >= 3.2 mm panel shift (+5 mm case diameter). Under the glass, the bend runs ~1.2-3.0 mm from the glass with inner R ~0.53 mm (Sharp min 0.45) |
| 2026-10-10 | Panel sits on a 1.2 mm spacer frame / foam ring at its edges (was 0.2 mm tape): +1.0 mm total thickness | Clears J2 and the parts under the panel (1.0 mm nominal, 1.1 mm max for J2 and the SC70 U7). The frame must carry the panel at its edges so the glass never presses on parts |
| 2026-10-10 | J2 on the front at 180 deg, pad row at y = +7.55 mm from centre (front view), opening toward 6 o'clock, Panasonic land pattern (pad n = LCD pin n, pad 1 = SCLK at +X) | Y5B takes the FPC on the pad side, 1.5 mm deep. The fold keeps left/right, so FPC pin 1 (right, display side up) stays at +X. The fold turns the tail over, so its contacts (on the FPC back, Sharp Fig. 4-1) face up, away from the PCB; that works because the Y5B has top and bottom contacts. FPC end at y ~5.75, stiffener flat from 5.75 to 10.25, ~0.8 mm spare tail (the fold can move 0.8-3.7 mm from the glass). FPC thickness at the stiffener 0.3 +/- 0.03 mm matches the Y5B |
| 2026-10-10 | Front, under the panel: J2, LCD buffers U6/U7 with C16-C20, the I2C/INT/GPO pull-ups R6, R7, R8, R14, and the NFC VCC decoupling C21. They sit over the cell area, off the FPC strip and clear of all drilled holes. All are 0402 (<= 0.55 mm) except U6 (1.0 mm max), U7 (1.1 mm max) and J2 (1.1 mm max) | The back ring was too crowded to route: parts in the coil ring can only escape on B.Cu, and the 6 o'clock and NFC corners choked. Over the cell the back has copper only at the BT1 pads, so front parts can drop vias anywhere. Front parts stay inside the coil (r <= 15.9 mm) |
| 2026-10-10 | Boost cluster placed and locked by hand: U3 at (-11.6, 9.4), L1 rotated so its SW pad faces U3 SW (single short track), C5/C6 output caps against VOUT with a direct GND return to U3 GND/EP and a GND via under the EP, R1/R2 at the FB pin | The autorouted version took SW, VIN, VOUT and GND through vias. Hand placement keeps the switching loop on one layer and a few mm long |
| 2026-10-10 | Routing (layout-4l): Freerouting 2.4.1 plus a grid router for the last few. 0 unrouted, DRC clean, schematic parity clean. 75 vias, ~930 mm of track. 37 short segments (12.4 mm total) are necked below 0.2 mm (>= 0.15 mm) to pass between pads | The worst detours are the LCD SPI lines at ~2x their minimum span (~7 mm extra each) and RTC_INT, all slow signals |
| 2026-10-10 | Assembly order for the LCD: with the panel standing off past 6 o'clock, display side DOWN (it flips over when folded), insert the FPC into J2 flat and close the lever; then fold the panel over onto the frame (180 deg under the glass, toward the back polariser, never toward the display side). One fold only: Sharp allows 3 bends max | Y5B lever must be closed before the panel covers it; Sharp LD-26X06A remarks 8-1 and 8-2 |
| 2026-10-10 | Drop the RTC cell-swap bridge: VBACKUP to VSS via 10k, switchover off (confirmed by Alex 2026-10-10) | No MLCC can be guaranteed by its IR spec to bridge a 60 s swap, and worst-case leakage would be 2–5.5 µA; a swap resets the time, NFC restores it, and the aging offset survives in EEPROM |
| 2026-10-10 | Case: hunter-style pocket watch, 43.9 mm x 10.8 mm. One-piece shell (back + middle), snap-in bezel with crystal, 1.2 mm panel frame, lid hinged at 9 on a 1 mm steel pin, snap catch at 3, bow lug at 12, clip-in cell door at 12 (model: case/eigentime32_case.py) | Built around the routed board; fit checked against the KiCad file by case/fitcheck.py |
| 2026-10-10 | Round window: Ø23.4 mm aperture centred on the LCD active area (1.85 mm above board centre) under a Ø26 x 1.0 mm flat mineral crystal (decided by Alex, 2026-10-10) | Classic look; off-the-shelf glass. The square's corners are hidden, so the watch face is drawn round |
| 2026-10-10 | Side buttons pressed through flexure tabs printed in the shell wall; ribs either side of each button body lock the board's rotation | No loose plungers; the tabs line up with the actuators by construction |
| 2026-10-10 | Lid sensing magnet: Ø3 x 1 mm N52 in the lid over U9 (~90 G at the sensor vs 9 G typ trip) | Big margin; the CT8132 axis is unconfirmed, so the pocket position is a parameter |

## Proposed, not yet confirmed
- RV-3032 CLKOUT at 1 Hz drives the LCD EXTCOMIN (through a buffer)
- ST25DV04K VCC switched from an MCU GPIO (PB0 on the schematic)
- ST's 10 µF VDD bulk (C14) left unfitted (DNP) because of its leakage
